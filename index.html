Learn software engineering with me

Personal, practical coaching from a practising engineer, not recorded lectures. We write real code, build real projects, and grow real skills in Java, Spring, microservices and the cloud.

1-on-1 mentoring

Private sessions built entirely around you: your goals, your pace, your projects. Ideal for students, career-switchers and junior developers who want focused guidance.

Engineer by craft, teacher at heart

I'm Gihan Munasinghe, a software engineer who loves the whole arc of building: from the first sketch of an idea, through clean architecture and well-tested code, to the moment real users get value from it.

I care about systems that are simple to reason about, resilient under pressure, and a pleasure to work in, and I care just as much about helping other engineers get there. That's the thread running through my ventures, my consulting, and the coaching I offer to individuals and small groups.
