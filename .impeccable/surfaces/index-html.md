# Surface brief: public site (homepage lead; ventures, shop, blog post, 404)

Scope: index.html is the lead surface; ventures.html, shop.html, blog/post.html and 404.html share its world. Visitor mode: Persuade (homepage, ventures), Read (blog post), Operate-light (shop). Admin panel out of scope.

Audience and job: balanced personal brand. Clients, learners, investors, readers and buyers must grasp who Gihan is and take one next step (email, WhatsApp, read, request a meeting, order). Proof: real photos, live posts/ventures/products, the restored CV timeline. Nothing invented.

Constraints: keep URLs, anchors, query params, SEO meta and JSON-LD, CSP, every API-driven feature and DOM ids that scripts use.

Degraded roll: concept-seed ran without challengers (network blocked); user chose the assigned direction over the interchange-map pick and the canon.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a paperback series and Gihan is its author. Each page is a volume with its own series colour; the homepage is the cover. It refuses the dark-gradient developer portfolio and its opposite, the cream editorial page.

OWN-WORLD: Flat print colour fields in horizontal tri-bands (cobalt blue for the author, orange for writing, bottle green for ventures, cerise red for the shop), cool off-white paper and blue-black ink, Archivo grotesque from condensed spine lettering to wide cover titles, Literata for running prose, a "G." roundel as the publisher's mark, hairline rules instead of cards, Phosphor icons.

STORY: The visitor meets a cover with a real face and name, learns the three roles from the blurb, browses a shelf of real titles, then reads the consulting contents, the teaching frontispiece, the author's history, and ends at the back cover with email and WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width cobalt top band carrying the nav (roundel left, links, "Work with me"), a tall paper middle band with "Gihan Munasinghe" at 5-6rem, the role line under a hairline, an 18-word blurb and Email / WhatsApp buttons on the left; the headshot as a mounted print on the right crossing all three bands; a cobalt bottom band with the roundel. Primary action: Email me.

FORM: Paperback series design (tri-band covers, Marber-grid rules, spines on a shelf); candidate 4 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key e48aeb2b. Signature interaction: the shelf, where spines slide out to show the cover on hover or focus. Motion grammar: things are placed (portrait settles onto the cover, books slot onto the shelf), exponential ease-out, nothing loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
